Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Solidauto ("we", "us") operates the Manage Files mobile application (the "App").

This page explains what information the App uses, what it sends off your device, and the choices you have. Your files stay on your device. The only information that leaves it is a message you choose to send us, and the update check the App makes with Google Play.

Your Files

The App uses storage access to list, open, organise, compress and share the files on your device. This happens entirely on your device, only for the actions you start. Your files and their names are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Information You Send Us

The in-app Contact Us form is optional. When you send a message, we receive:

  • What you enter: the topic you choose (bug report, feature request, question or praise), your message, your email address and, if you give one, a star rating.
  • Technical details: the App version and build number, Android version and device model, so we can reproduce problems you report.

We use this information only to reply to you and to fix and improve the App. It is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our feedback service, which is hosted on Vercel. We do not sell it, share it with third parties for advertising, or add you to any mailing list. We keep messages only as long as we need them to handle your request, and delete them sooner if you ask.

App Updates

The App asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, and installs it through Google Play if you choose to update. That check is handled by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy; no information from it is sent to us.

What the App Does Not Do

The App has no advertising, analytics, tracking, crash reporting or user accounts.

When you use Share or Open With, the file is handed to the app you pick. What that app does with it is governed by its own privacy policy.

Permissions Requested

  • All files access (Manage all files): required to browse and manage storage outside the App's own folder. This is the core functionality of a file manager.
  • Photos, videos and music (Android 13 and later): used to show your media if you do not grant All files access.
  • Install unknown apps: used only when you tap an APK file and choose to install it.
  • Internet: used only to send messages through Contact Us and to check Google Play for updates.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us a message, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Choices

You can use every feature of the App without sending us anything. To access or delete a message you have sent, email us from the address you used.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page, update the "Last updated" date above and include the new text in the App.
